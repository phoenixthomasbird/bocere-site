What it does.

Write

  • A Word-style editor on a book-shaped page
  • Chapters you drag to reorder; a Journal that is never published
  • Front matter that fills itself in: title page, copyright, contents
  • Existing books are detected the first time you open them
  • A 90-day writing challenge; right-to-left languages such as Hebrew

Proof and publish

  • Print Proof with KDP’s checks: page minimum, gutter, spine
  • Print-ready PDF and an EPUB 3 file for Kindle
  • Paperback in 16 KDP trims and hardcover in 5
  • Book Details laid out like KDP’s form, with a Copy button on each field
  • A cover image size check before you upload

Work with an editor

  • Send your book as one file; your editor opens it in Bōcere
  • Their changes come back page by page as tracked changes
  • Accept or reject each one
  • Production notes you share; a journal only you see

Never lose writing

  • Atomic saves and recovery copies
  • Timestamped snapshots, never pruned
  • Your book is a plain folder you can back up anywhere
  • The cover designer (Pro, or $20 per book): a cover that updates from your Book Details

Questions.

Does Scribe have an AI writing assistant?

Not on author plans at launch. Scribe is a writing and formatting app; your editor’s changes arrive as tracked changes you accept or reject.

Can I bring a book I already started?

Yes. Open the folder and Scribe works out which files are chapters, which is the journal and which pages are front matter, then saves that so you can adjust it.

Do I upload to Amazon from the app?

No. Scribe makes the files; you upload them to Kindle Direct Publishing yourself.

What happens if I stop paying for Pro?

Your books and files stay on your computer. Pro features (every theme, hardcover, the cover designer) are available while you subscribe.
