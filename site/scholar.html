III

What it does.

Books

  • Everything in Bōcere Scribe
  • Bookshelf with Drafts, Proofs and Published shelves
  • Chapters, Book Details and Cover, as in Scribe

Blogs

  • One tile per blog; keep separate voices separate
  • Post Details: title, slug, excerpt with a character count, tags, hero image
  • Preview at your site’s width, in your site’s font
  • Publish to site sends the post as an unpublished draft to Shopify, WordPress or a static site in a Git repository. You publish it there.

Newsletters

  • Issues with subject line (with a length count) and preview text
  • A List tab connected to your Buttondown account
  • Send creates the issue as a draft at Buttondown; you send or schedule it there
  • The app never sends email on its own

One dashboard

  • Recent: the book, blog and newsletter you last opened
  • Waiting on you: reviews back from your editor, posts and issues in review
  • What’s due next, across every project
  • Service keys kept in your system keychain, never in your folders

Pricing on request.
