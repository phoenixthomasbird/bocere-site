The AI guides. You write. Every word in the essay came from your keyboard.

Bōcere Scribe

A maker of books, bound for the printer.

Write, edit and format your book on your own computer. KDP-ready when you are.

Questions.

Does Bōcere write my book or essay for me?

No. Bōcere is where you write. Changes from an editor or an AI arrive as suggestions you accept or reject, and Student never lets an AI type into your essay.

Where are my files kept?

On your computer, as plain Markdown files: one file per essay in Student, a folder of chapters per book in Scribe and Scholar. You can open them in any text editor.

Which computers does it run on?

Mac and Windows. Bōcere is a desktop app, not a website.

Is Bōcere connected to Amazon?

No. Scribe makes files that meet Kindle Direct Publishing’s requirements; you upload them to KDP yourself.
